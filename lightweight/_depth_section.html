Overall Sentiment -
💡 Bars (right scale): total traded volume (buyVol + sellVol), tinted green/red by which side was aggressing.
💡 MA Total Volume (yellow): moving average of total volume — this is what makes "was there volume?" a checkable question.
💡 CVD cumulative (white): running delta sum with no reset. This is the line that shows price/CVD divergence.
💡 Rolling Δ (cyan): net delta over the last N bars — an oscillator. It cannot show divergence; the window discards the memory divergence needs.
💡 Buy/Sell Ratio (purple, left scale): taker buy/sell ratio. >1 = aggressive buying.
💡 Top Traders: T-Position/Account/G-Account show sentiment of top traders and overall market. >1 = bullish.
